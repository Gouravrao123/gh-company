Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data GH Company collects through this website, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices and rights available to you.

1. Who we are

GH Company is a digital marketing agency based in Rewari, Haryana, India. For the personal data described in this Privacy Policy, GH Company decides how and why that information is used.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us:

  • Email: ghcompanyofficial@gmail.com
  • Phone: +91 7206718075
  • WhatsApp: Message GH Company

2. What personal data we collect

When you contact us through our website forms or WhatsApp, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name
  • Your business name, if provided
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address, if provided
  • The service you are interested in
  • The message or enquiry you send us

Please do not include passwords, bank details, identity document numbers or other sensitive information in your messages.

3. When we collect your information

We collect the information described above when you:

  • Submit the free audit form on this website.
  • Submit the contact form on this website.
  • Send us a message through WhatsApp.

4. Why we use your personal data

We use the information you provide for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry or request.
  • To prepare your free website or business audit.
  • To prepare a quotation or service proposal.
  • To follow up about the specific request you submitted.

5. Our promise on the use of your information

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

We use the information for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Your consent

Our website forms include a consent checkbox that you must select yourself before submitting the form. It is not pre-selected, and the form is not submitted without the required consent.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing ghcompanyofficial@gmail.com with the subject line "Withdraw consent", or by contacting us through WhatsApp.

After you withdraw consent, we stop using your personal data and delete it unless the law requires us to retain it. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before your withdrawal.

If you do not wish to submit a website form, you can still contact us directly by phone, WhatsApp or email.

7. Who receives your data

To operate this website and respond to enquiries, certain service providers may receive or process information:

  • Web3Forms: A form service that delivers website form submissions to our email.
  • Email provider: The provider through which we receive and read enquiries.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your communication is handled through that platform.
  • Google Fonts: When the page loads fonts from Google Fonts, Google may receive your IP address and browser-related information.
  • Website hosting provider: Our hosting provider maintains standard server logs as part of operating the website.

8. How long we keep your information

We retain personal data only for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and maintain related business records.

You can contact us to request deletion of your personal data. We may retain information where applicable law requires us to do so.

9. How we protect your information

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. These include:

  • Using HTTPS on our website.
  • Collecting only the information needed for the purposes described in this policy.
  • Limiting access to the email account that receives enquiries.
  • Protecting access to that email account.

No online system or method of electronic storage is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects your information, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by applicable law.

10. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate information.
  • Request erasure of your personal data.
  • Withdraw your consent.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you are unable to do so.
  • Make a complaint about how your personal data is handled.

11. How to exercise your rights or make a complaint

To make a privacy-related request, email us at ghcompanyofficial@gmail.com.

We will respond within the time limit set by applicable law. If you are not satisfied with how your concern is handled, you may make a complaint to the Data Protection Board of India as provided under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

12. Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18.

If you believe that a child has sent us personal data, please contact us at ghcompanyofficial@gmail.com so that we can review the matter and delete the information where appropriate.

13. Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently set cookies or use advertising or analytics trackers.

It stores one temporary item in your browser's session storage only to prevent the free audit popup from appearing again during your visit.

If we introduce analytics or advertising trackers in the future, we will ask for your consent first.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page, and the date at the top will show when it was last updated.
