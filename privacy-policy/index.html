Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. What personal data GH Company collects through this website, why we collect it, and your choices and rights.

Who we are

GH Company is a digital marketing agency based in Rewari, Haryana, India. For the personal data described here, GH Company decides how and why it is used. Contact: ghcompanyofficial@gmail.com, phone and WhatsApp +91 7206718075.

What personal data we collect

  • Your name
  • Your business name (optional)
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address (optional)
  • The service you are interested in
  • The message you write to us

When we collect it

Only when you submit the free audit form or the contact form on this website, or when you message us on WhatsApp. Please do not include passwords, bank details or ID numbers in your message.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry
  • To prepare your free audit or a quotation
  • To follow up about that request

Our promise on use

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Your consent

Our forms have a consent box that you must tick yourself. It is never pre-ticked, and we do not send the form without it. You can withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by emailing ghcompanyofficial@gmail.com with the subject Withdraw consent, or by messaging us on WhatsApp. After you withdraw, we stop using your data and delete it unless the law requires us to keep it. Withdrawal does not affect what we did before. If you do not consent, you can still contact us directly by phone, WhatsApp or email.

Who receives your data

  • Web3Forms, a form service that delivers your form submission to our email
  • Our email provider, where we receive and read enquiries
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you message us there
  • Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details when the page loads fonts
  • Our website hosting provider, which keeps standard server logs

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed to answer your enquiry and for related business records. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

How we protect it

The site uses HTTPS, we collect only what we need, and access to the email account that receives enquiries is limited and protected. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Your rights

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or update it
  • Ask us to erase it
  • Withdraw your consent
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you are unable to
  • Make a complaint about how we handle your data

How to use your rights or complain

Email ghcompanyofficial@gmail.com. We will reply within the time limit set by law. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India as provided under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent us data, email us and we will delete it.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently set cookies or use advertising or analytics trackers. It stores one temporary item in your browser (session storage) only so that the free audit popup does not appear again during your visit. If we add analytics or ads in future, we will ask for your consent first.

Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
