How digital marketing agency pricing works

Agencies usually charge in one of three ways. Monthly retainer: a fixed fee each month for ongoing work such as SEO, social media or ads management. Project fee: a one-time price for a defined piece of work such as a website. Per deliverable: a price for each unit of work. Advertising budget is separate from all of these, because it is paid to the advertising platform and not to the agency.

What is not included in these prices

  • Ad spend. Money paid to Meta or other platforms for running ads.
  • Domain and hosting for your website, which are renewed yearly with the providers.
  • Taxes such as GST, where applicable.
  • Paid tools, stock footage or licensed music if you request them.
  • Printing and outdoor media.

What changes the price

  • The number of platforms, services and locations.
  • How many posts, reels, pages or videos you need each month.
  • How competitive your industry and city are.
  • The current state of your website, profiles and content.
  • How much video production is needed compared with editing existing footage.
  • The depth of reporting and strategy you want.

How to compare quotes from any agency

  1. Ask for a written scope: platforms, number of deliverables, reports and timelines.
  2. Check what is excluded: ad spend, tools, domain, hosting and taxes.
  3. Ask about the minimum contract period and the notice period.
  4. Ask who will own your accounts, files and profiles.
  5. Do not compare only the total. Compare what you get for it.
  6. Be careful with anyone who guarantees rankings, leads or sales.
