How digital marketing agency pricing works

Digital marketing agencies generally use different pricing models depending on the type of service.

  • Monthly retainer: A recurring fee for ongoing services such as SEO, social media management and advertising campaign management.
  • Project fee: A one-time or milestone-based fee for defined work such as website development.
  • Per deliverable: A fee based on specific items of work, such as individual videos, designs or other creative assets.

Advertising spend is separate from the agency's management fee. It is paid to the advertising platform, not to the agency.

What is not included in these prices?

Unless specifically included in your written quotation, the following costs are separate:

  • Ad spend: Money paid directly to Meta or other advertising platforms to run campaigns.
  • Domain and hosting: Website domain registration and hosting renewals charged by the respective providers.
  • Taxes: Applicable taxes such as GST, where applicable.
  • Paid tools and licensed assets: Paid software, stock footage or licensed music if required.
  • Printing and outdoor media: Physical advertising and printing costs.

What changes the price?

The final cost of digital marketing services can depend on several factors:

  • The number of services, platforms and target locations.
  • The number of posts, reels, pages or videos required.
  • Competition in your industry and target market.
  • The existing condition of your website, profiles and content.
  • The amount of new video production required compared with editing existing footage.
  • The level of strategy, reporting and campaign management needed.

How to compare quotes from any digital marketing agency

Before choosing an agency, compare the actual work included in the quotation rather than looking only at the total price.

  1. Ask for a written scope covering platforms, deliverables, reports and timelines.
  2. Check what is excluded, including ad spend, tools, domain, hosting and taxes.
  3. Ask about the minimum contract period and notice period.
  4. Confirm who owns your advertising accounts, website, creative files and social profiles.
  5. Compare the deliverables and service quality, not only the final amount.
  6. Be careful with anyone who guarantees rankings, leads or sales.
