Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) Management in Rewari & Haryana

Facebook and Instagram advertising planned and managed to bring you enquiries, not just views.

What are Meta ads?

Meta ads are paid advertisements shown on Facebook and Instagram. You choose who sees them, such as people in a particular area, age group or interest, and you pay according to your budget and campaign settings. Because the targeting is precise, Meta ads can bring quicker results than organic posting, especially for local businesses with a clear offer.

What we handle

  • Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns: setup, targeting, budget planning and launch.
  • Lead generation campaigns: ads designed to collect enquiries through forms, calls or WhatsApp messages.
  • Retargeting: ads for people who already visited your website or engaged with your profile.
  • Campaign optimization: regular review of what is working and adjustments to audience, creative and budget.

Before we launch a campaign

  • Goal: we decide what counts as success, such as enquiries, calls or website visits.
  • Audience: we define who should see the ads and where.
  • Creative: we prepare the images, videos and ad text.
  • Destination: we confirm where people will land, such as a form, a WhatsApp chat or a page on your website.
  • Tracking: we set up the tracking needed to measure results properly.

How campaigns are optimized

After launch we watch the numbers available in the ad account, such as reach, clicks and enquiries. We test different creatives and audiences, pause what is not working and put more budget behind what is. Early weeks are mostly about learning, so we do not judge a campaign too quickly.

Ads work better with a strong base

An ad can bring people to you, but your page or website decides whether they take action. A clear landing page, quality creatives and a regular social media presence all improve results. For long-term, free traffic as well, add SEO.

Honest expectations

We cannot promise a fixed number of leads or a fixed cost per lead, because results depend on your offer, competition, location and budget. We agree the plan, budget and reporting format before launch, and we share what the data shows.

PPC advertising guide: Meta ads and Google ads

PPC means you pay the platform when people see or click your ads, depending on the platform and settings. Google Search ads reach people who are typing a search right now. Meta ads on Facebook and Instagram reach people based on location, interests and behaviour, even before they start searching. Both can work for local businesses. Our current managed advertising service is Meta Ads Management. If Google Search ads would suit you better, we will tell you honestly during the discovery conversation.

Frequently asked questions

Yes. Campaigns can be set up so people message you directly on WhatsApp.

Some campaigns bring enquiries within days, but the first weeks are used for testing and improving.

Not always. A simple landing page or a lead form can work, and we will advise you on the best option.
