What Are Meta Ads?

Meta Ads are paid advertisements displayed across platforms such as Facebook and Instagram. Businesses can use them to reach audiences based on factors such as location, interests and campaign settings. With the right audience, creative and offer, Meta advertising can help local businesses generate awareness, website visits, calls and customer enquiries.

For businesses in Rewari and across Haryana, Facebook and Instagram advertising can be useful for promoting local services, products, offers, admissions and business campaigns.

Meta Ads Management Services We Provide

GH Company helps businesses plan, launch and manage Facebook and Instagram advertising campaigns based on their marketing goals.

  • Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns: Campaign setup, audience targeting, budget planning and launch.
  • Lead generation campaigns: Advertising designed to generate enquiries through forms, calls or WhatsApp messages.
  • Retargeting campaigns: Ads for eligible audiences who have previously visited your website or engaged with your business profile.
  • Campaign optimization: Regular review and adjustments to audience, creative, placements and budget.
  • Ad creative and copy: Direction for images, videos, headlines and advertising messages.

What We Check Before Launching a Campaign

  • Campaign goal: Decide what success means, such as enquiries, calls, messages or website visits.
  • Target audience: Identify the relevant customers, locations and audience settings.
  • Ad creative: Prepare suitable images, videos and advertising copy.
  • Destination: Confirm where people will go after clicking, such as a landing page, lead form or WhatsApp chat.
  • Tracking: Set up the appropriate measurement tools to understand campaign performance.

How We Optimize Facebook and Instagram Ads

Launching an ad campaign is only the beginning. We review the available campaign data, including reach, impressions, clicks, cost and enquiries. Based on the results, we test different creatives and audiences, pause underperforming variations and adjust budgets where the data supports a change.

The initial weeks are important for testing and learning. We avoid making decisions based on a small amount of data and review campaign performance against the agreed goal.

PPC Advertising: Meta Ads vs Google Ads

PPC stands for pay-per-click, although advertising platforms can use different billing and optimization models depending on campaign settings. Google Search Ads can reach people actively searching for products or services, while Meta Ads on Facebook and Instagram can reach audiences based on factors such as location, interests and behaviour.

Both platforms can support local business marketing, but the right choice depends on customer intent, competition, budget and campaign objectives.
