Social Media Marketing & Management in Rewari

A consistent, well-planned Instagram and Facebook presence for your business, handled by a team that understands both content and strategy.

Why a consistent social presence matters

Before people call or visit, many check your social media profile. A regular, clear and well-designed profile shows that your business is active and trustworthy. An empty or inconsistent one raises doubts. Social media also keeps existing customers interested and gives new people a reason to remember you.

The challenge for most owners is time. Good content needs ideas, design, editing, captions and regular posting. We take that work off your hands.

What we manage

  • Content calendar: a monthly plan of topics, formats and dates, shared before posting begins.
  • Post and reel planning: ideas for posts, carousels, reels and stories that fit your brand and audience.
  • Social media account management: scheduling, publishing and keeping your profiles organized.
  • Profile setup and optimization: clear bio, profile image, highlights and links that tell visitors what to do next.

How the monthly workflow runs

  • Planning: we agree the goals, themes and number of posts for the month.
  • Calendar approval: you review the calendar and tell us what to change.
  • Content creation: designs, captions and reel edits are prepared by our team.
  • Publishing: approved content is posted on schedule.
  • Monthly review: we look at what performed well and improve the next plan.

Content that fits your business

A coaching institute needs different content from a gym or a local shop. We build a few content themes around your business, such as results and learning, behind the scenes, offers, tips and customer questions, and rotate them so your feed stays varied but focused. For video content we work with our creative content production service.

Social media and your other marketing

Social media works best alongside other channels. We can connect it with Meta ads to reach new audiences, and with your website so interested people have somewhere clear to go. If you are starting from scratch, our digital marketing strategy helps decide how much to invest in each.

What we need from you

Basic details about your business, access to your profiles, photos or videos you already have, and quick approvals on the monthly calendar. The more you share about your customers and offers, the better the content becomes.

Choosing the right platforms for your business

You do not need to be on every platform. Instagram suits visual businesses such as gyms, salons, cafes and coaching institutes. Facebook still works well for local communities, events and parents. YouTube Shorts can repurpose your reels. We help you choose the platforms where your customers actually spend time and do those well, rather than spreading thin.

A sample month of content (concept)

This is an illustrative plan, not a client result. Our standard monthly service typically covers around 16 posts and 8 reels with a strategy and a monthly report, and the mix changes with your business.

  • Week 1: introduce the business, one educational reel, one behind-the-scenes post, one offer.
  • Week 2: answer the three most common customer questions as short reels or carousels.
  • Week 3: show real work or a process with captions, and run one poll in Stories.
  • Week 4: share a useful checklist, a seasonal offer and a recap of the month.

Metrics that matter and metrics that mislead

Followers and likes are easy to count but weak signals of business. We report them, but we pay more attention to profile visits, link clicks, saves and shares, direction requests, calls and WhatsApp messages. Our guide on common digital marketing mistakes explains why chasing followers can mislead.

Frequently asked questions

Which platforms do you manage?

We focus on Instagram and Facebook, where most local businesses find their customers.

Do I approve posts before they go live?

Yes. You review the content calendar and can request changes before anything is published.

How many posts will I get each month?

The number is agreed in advance and written into your plan, based on your goals and budget.

Can you also run ads on my profile?

Yes, through our Meta Ads Management service, which is planned separately.

How many posts do I need each month?

It depends on your goals and capacity. A steady rhythm you can keep for at least 90 days matters more than a high number. Our standard plan typically covers around 16 posts and 8 reels per month.

Do you handle ads as well?

Paid campaigns on Facebook and Instagram are a separate service, Meta Ads Management, and can be combined with social media management.

Can you work in Hindi or Hinglish?

Yes. We can write captions and subtitles in English, Hindi or Hinglish, depending on your audience.

What do your social media management services cost?

Social Media Management is ₹20,000 per month. The final price is confirmed after a discovery conversation.
