Video Editing & Graphic Design Services in Rewari

Professional Instagram reels, promotional videos, graphic designs and branded content for businesses in Rewari, Haryana. Create content that communicates clearly, builds trust and supports your marketing goals.

Video editing and graphic design for growing businesses

Your online presence is often the first impression customers have of your business. Clear visuals, well-edited videos and consistent branding help people understand what you offer and make your business look professional.

GH Company provides video editing and graphic design services for local businesses, schools, coaching institutes, gyms, retailers and growing brands. Whether you need regular Instagram content or a promotional campaign, we plan creative work around your audience and business objectives.

Why creative quality matters

People decide quickly whether a business looks reliable. Clear graphics, well-edited videos and a consistent visual identity make your business feel organised and professional, while rushed or mismatched content can weaken that impression.

Creative quality also influences how effectively a social media post or advertisement communicates its message. Good content should not only look attractive; it should help the viewer understand the offer and know what to do next.

Our video editing and graphic design services

  • Instagram Reels Editing: short vertical videos with clean cuts, captions, music and a clear message.
  • Promotional Video Editing: videos for offers, launches, admissions, services, products and events.
  • Graphic Designing: social media posts, carousel designs, banners, story creatives and advertising graphics.
  • Brand Content: templates, highlight covers and consistent visual styles for your social media presence.
  • Short-Form Video Content: engaging video formats for Instagram, Facebook and YouTube Shorts.

How our creative content process works

  • Brief: You tell us the goal, audience and message of the content.
  • Concept: We suggest the format, visual style and content structure.
  • Creation: We design or edit the content using your raw footage, photos and brand details.
  • Review and revisions: You review the draft and we make the agreed improvements.
  • Final delivery: We deliver files in the formats required for your selected platforms.

What we need from you

To create content that matches your business, we may need your logo, brand colours, raw video clips or photographs, the message you want to communicate and any design references you like.

If you do not have existing footage, we can help plan simple shooting ideas that you can record using your phone. Clear, steady footage and good lighting are often enough to get started with short-form videos.

Creative content for different marketing goals

Content designed for brand awareness is different from content designed to generate enquiries. We choose the format and message according to what you want your audience to do.

  • Brand awareness: introductory reels, business showcases and brand story creatives.
  • Engagement: educational carousels, FAQs, polls and relatable short videos.
  • Enquiries: service explainers, promotional graphics and clear call-to-action videos.
  • Advertising: ad creatives designed around the campaign message and target audience.

Our creative services also support your Social Media Management and Meta Ads Management campaigns. You can order creative work separately or include it in a wider marketing plan.

Quality, revisions and delivery

Before starting a larger project, we agree on the number of videos or designs, revision rounds and delivery timelines in writing. This helps both sides understand the scope of work before production begins.

You can review our sample work and creative concepts to understand our visual approach before discussing your project.

Reel formats that work for local businesses

  • Problem and solution: Open with a relatable customer problem, then show how your business solves it.
  • Process reels: Give viewers a short look at how your service or product is made or delivered.
  • FAQ reels: Answer one common customer question with a simple and clear explanation.
  • Before and after: Show genuine changes or results, only with the customer's permission.
  • Offer and announcement reels: Share clear details, dates and one relevant call to action.

From idea to final edit: our workflow

  1. Brief: Understand your goal, audience, offer and available footage.
  2. Script and storyboard: Prepare a short outline of the hook, scenes and call to action.
  3. Edit: Add cuts, transitions, music, captions and colour adjustments that match your brand.
  4. Review: Share the draft for feedback and make changes within the agreed revision scope.
  5. Export and delivery: Deliver files in suitable sizes and formats for Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.

Brand consistency across every creative

Consistent colours, fonts, logo placement and communication style make your posts recognisable. A consistent visual identity also helps customers connect different pieces of content with the same business.

For new brands, we can create a visual direction using logo design, colours, fonts and brand guidelines. If your business already has an established identity, we work within your existing brand guidelines.

Hindi, Hinglish and English subtitles

Readable subtitles help viewers who watch videos without sound and make content easier to follow for people who prefer their own language.

We can create content in Hindi, English or Hinglish, depending on your target audience. Subtitles are styled to match your brand while remaining readable and within the safe areas of each platform.

What to prepare before your project starts

  • Raw video clips or photographs, even if recorded on a phone.
  • Your logo and brand colours, if available.
  • A short list of the key points you want the content to communicate.
  • Examples of videos or designs you like.
  • Any dates, prices, offers or business details that must appear in the creative.
