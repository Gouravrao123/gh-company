How We Approach a Digital Marketing Project

Every project starts with three important questions: who is the target customer, what is the business offer and how will success be measured? These illustrative plans show how we translate business goals into a structured first month of digital marketing activity.

The plans below are illustrative concepts. They are not actual client projects and do not represent verified business results.
Concept plan

Digital Marketing Plan for a Neighbourhood Gym

Goal: Generate trial-session enquiries.
Week 1: Complete Google Business Profile information and collect genuine business photos.
Week 2: Introduce trainers and fitness programmes through short reels.
Week 3: Publish member Q&A content and test a small Meta Ads campaign.
Week 4: Review enquiries and adjust the campaign strategy.

Concept plan

Digital Marketing Plan for a Coaching Institute

Goal: Generate admission enquiries before a new batch begins.
Week 1: Improve search visibility and batch information pages.
Week 2: Publish teaching clips and parent FAQs.
Week 3: Create seasonal content with clear fee and timing information.
Week 4: Establish an enquiry follow-up routine and review performance.

Concept plan

Digital Marketing Plan for a Local Retail Shop

Goal: Increase calls and physical store visits.
Week 1: Improve Google Business Profile details, business hours and directions.
Week 2: Plan a simple business website with a WhatsApp contact option.
Week 3: Create product-focused and promotional reels.
Week 4: Track calls, messages and customer visits.
