How we approach a project

Every project starts from the same questions: who is the customer, what is the offer and how will we count results? The concept plans below show how that thinking turns into a first month of work.

The plans below are illustrative concepts. They are not client work and they do not describe real results.
Concept plan

A neighbourhood gym

Goal: trial-session enquiries.
Week 1: complete Google profile, collect real photos.
Week 2: introduce trainers and programmes in short reels.
Week 3: member Q&A and a small Meta ads test.
Week 4: review enquiries and adjust.

Concept plan

A coaching institute

Goal: admission enquiries before a new batch.
Week 1: clean up search presence and batch pages.
Week 2: teaching clips and parent FAQs.
Week 3: seasonal content with clear fee and timing information.
Week 4: follow-up routine and review.

Concept plan

A local retail shop

Goal: calls and walk-ins.
Week 1: Google profile, hours and directions.
Week 2: simple website with WhatsApp button.
Week 3: product and offer reels.
Week 4: count calls, messages and visits.

  • Real work that you can open or see, with the client’s permission.

  • Honest labels for concept or sample work.
  • Results described with the numbers and the way they were measured, not only adjectives.
