Enterprise SEO Strategy: How to Compare Agencies, Hire One and Understand SEO Consulting Pricing

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 6 November 2026

Enterprise SEO is SEO for large or complex websites: hundreds of pages, many locations, several teams. This guide covers an enterprise SEO strategy, an enterprise SEO agency comparison, how to hire an enterprise SEO agency and how SEO consulting pricing works.

What enterprise SEO means

The basics are the same as any SEO, but scale changes the work. A growing business with branches, a big catalogue or many service pages needs rules, templates and clear ownership so that thousands of small decisions stay consistent. If you are earlier in the journey, start with our small business SEO tips first.

An enterprise SEO strategy in six steps

  1. Audit first. Check crawling, indexing, speed, duplicate pages and thin pages before writing anything new.
  2. Fix the structure. Clean URLs, clear categories and internal links so important pages are easy to reach.
  3. Map keywords to pages. One main topic per page, so your own pages do not compete with each other.
  4. Use templates carefully. Location and product pages need real, different information. Copy-paste pages hurt; see why copy-paste location pages fail.
  5. Set ownership. Decide who approves content, who fixes technical issues and how often reports are reviewed.
  6. Measure what matters. Track impressions, clicks, calls, form fills and enquiries, not only rankings.

Enterprise SEO agency comparison: what to look at

When you compare enterprise SEO agencies, look beyond the pitch deck:

  • Process. Can they explain how an audit becomes a prioritised plan?
  • Reporting. Do reports show enquiries and business results, or only rankings and traffic? Read what unclear agency reporting looks like.
  • Access and ownership. Your accounts and data should stay in your name. See why agencies should never own your accounts.
  • Honesty about results. Nobody can guarantee rankings. Treat that promise as a warning sign; our guide on guarantees explains why.
  • Team fit. Who will actually do the work, and how quickly do they reply?

How to hire an enterprise SEO agency

Before you hire enterprise SEO agency support, write down your goal (for example, more enquiries from three cities), share your current site and access, and ask each agency for a short plan and a written scope. Compare plans, not only prices. Our step-by-step process to hire a digital marketing agency works for SEO too.

SEO consulting pricing: what it depends on

SEO consulting pricing is usually a monthly retainer, a one-time audit or a mix of both. The main cost drivers are the size of the site, the number of locations, how competitive your keywords are, how much content is needed and how much technical work is involved. Be careful with very low or very high quotes that do not explain the scope. See our pricing guide and hidden costs to ask about.

Frequently asked questions

What is enterprise SEO?

SEO for large or complex websites, with many pages, locations or teams, where structure, templates and ownership matter as much as keywords.

How much does SEO consulting cost?

It depends on site size, competition and scope. Ask for a written scope and compare what is included, not only the price.

Can an agency guarantee rankings?

No. Search engines decide rankings. A good agency commits to clear work and honest reporting instead.
