Digital Marketing Consultation: What to Expect and How to Request a Marketing Proposal

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

A good first conversation saves time and money. Here is what a digital marketing consultation should cover, how to prepare and how to request a marketing proposal.

What a digital marketing consultation covers

  • Your business, customers and goals.
  • What you have tried and what happened.
  • A review of your website, profiles and competitors.
  • Options that fit your budget.
  • Next steps and what a proposal would include.

How to prepare

  • Write down your top goal for the next 90 days.
  • Collect your website, social and Google profile links.
  • Note your rough budget range.
  • List your questions about scope, reporting and terms.

Request marketing proposal: the steps

  1. Contact us on WhatsApp or through the contact form.
  2. Share your business type, city, links and main goal.
  3. Join a short discovery conversation.
  4. Receive a written proposal with scope, deliverables, timeline and price.
  5. Compare it with others and decide.

Hire digital marketing agency support: what a good proposal contains

  • Goals and how progress will be measured.
  • Services and deliverables with numbers.
  • Timeline and first-month plan.
  • Price with exclusions listed.
  • Contract terms and ownership of accounts.

Why choose a full-service digital marketing agency

A full-service digital marketing agency plans SEO, social media, creative content, ads and your website together, so they support each other. GH Company offers all six services separately or as the All Services Package with 10% off. See digital marketing agency pricing and our services overview.

A worked example (concept)

A gym owner prepares three things before the call: the goal (more trial sign-ups), links to the website and Instagram, and a rough monthly budget. After the call the owner receives a written proposal with deliverables, a first-month plan and a price, and compares it with another quote before deciding.

Questions to ask on the call

  1. How would you approach my goal?
  2. What will you do first?
  3. How will you report?
  4. What is excluded from the price?
  5. What do you need from me?
