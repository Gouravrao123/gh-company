Does Your Agency Own Your Accounts? Domain, Ads, Google Profile and Pages

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

If the domain, ad account or Google profile is in the agency’s name, your business can lose access when the relationship ends. Here is how to protect yourself.

Key takeaways

  • Ask for regular exports

What this frustration looks like

  • The domain or hosting is registered in the agency’s name.
  • You do not have admin access to your ad account.
  • Your Google Business Profile is managed from the agency’s login.
  • Social pages were created under a personal account of the agency.

Why it happens

It is faster for agencies to create everything under their own accounts. Sometimes it is carelessness, sometimes a way to keep clients.

What it costs you

When ownership sits elsewhere, leaving becomes difficult. You may lose your website, reviews, followers or ad history.

How to avoid it

  1. List every asset: domain, hosting, website files, Google profile, ad account, social pages, analytics, content files.
  2. Make your business the owner of each, with the agency added as a manager or user.
  3. Keep login details secure and recorded.
  4. Ask for regular exports or backups of important files.
  5. Include ownership in writing in the agreement.
  6. Check access yourself every few months.

Questions to ask any agency

  • Who will own each of these assets?
  • Will I have admin access?
  • How will handover work if I leave?
  • Where are files stored?

A concept example

A salon’s Instagram page was created on an agency staff member’s personal profile. When the staff member left, the salon could not recover it. Owning the page as the business would have avoided this. This is an illustration, not a client result.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Giving passwords instead of adding users.
  • Not recording domain details.
  • Assuming reviews belong to the agency.
  • Ignoring Google Business Profile ownership.

Myths vs reality

MythReality
The agency needs to own it to manage itManagers and users can do the work without ownership
It is too technical to checkOwnership can be checked in minutes
Only websites matterEvery account matters

A 30-day action plan

  1. Week 1: Make a list of your accounts and who owns each.
  2. Week 2: Fix any that are not in your name.
  3. Week 3: Add your agency as a manager where needed.
  4. Week 4: Re-check quarterly.

How to measure improvement

You will know it is working when you can log in as owner to every account that represents your business without asking anyone.

Frequently asked questions

Who should own the domain?

Your business, registered with your details.

Can an agency manage a Google profile I own?

Yes, as a manager or user added by you.

What if I do not own something already?

Ask the agency to transfer it and record the process in writing.
