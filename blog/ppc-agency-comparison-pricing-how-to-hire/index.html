PPC Agency Comparison and Pricing: How to Hire a PPC Agency Without Wasting Budget

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

Choosing someone to run your ads is a big decision. This guide covers PPC agency comparison, what Google Ads management services include, how pricing works and what to ask for in an audit.

What a PPC management company does

A PPC management company plans, launches and optimizes pay-per-click campaigns. Typical work includes goal setting, keyword and audience research, ad creation, landing page advice, tracking setup, weekly optimization and reporting. Ad spend itself is paid to the platform.

Google Ads management services: what to expect

  • A written plan with goals, budget and timeline.
  • Account setup with conversion tracking.
  • Keyword and negative keyword management.
  • Ad copy testing.
  • Regular optimization and a clear report.
  • Advice on the landing page and follow-up.

Paid media agency pricing and Google Ads management pricing

Pricing varies a lot between agencies. Common models are a flat monthly fee, a percentage of ad spend or a mix. Ad spend is separate and goes to the platform, and taxes and tools may be extra. Always ask for a written breakdown. Treat any online average as rough context because the right price depends on your scope and competition.

Hire PPC agency support: steps

  1. Define your goal and budget.
  2. Shortlist two or three agencies.
  3. Hold discovery calls and compare answers.
  4. Request a written proposal.
  5. Check who will own the accounts.
  6. Start with a limited scope and review.

PPC audit request: what to ask for

If you already run ads, you can request a PPC audit from any provider. A good audit looks at goals and tracking, search terms and negative keywords, ad copy, audiences, landing pages, budgets and results, and ends with a prioritised list of actions in plain language. Ask what will be reviewed, how long it takes and whether it is free or paid before you agree.

A worked example (concept)

An owner receives two proposals. One offers a low monthly fee but includes no reporting and keeps the ad account under the agency’s name. The other costs more, lists deliverables, reports enquiries and cost per enquiry, and creates the ad account under the business. After building a comparison table, the owner chooses on clarity and ownership, not price alone.

Questions to ask before you hire

  1. What will you do in the first 30 days?
  2. Which numbers will you report, and how often?
  3. Who owns the ad account and data?
  4. What is excluded from the fee: ad spend, tools, taxes?
  5. What is the minimum term and notice period?
  6. Can I speak to a current client or see live work?

Frequently asked questions

How do I compare PPC agencies?

Compare how they start, what they report, who owns the accounts, what they promise and how pricing and terms are shown.

Is a percentage of ad spend a fair fee?

It can be, but it can also encourage higher spend. Ask for a clear scope and compare it with a flat fee.
