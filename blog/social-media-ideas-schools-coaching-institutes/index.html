Social Media Content Ideas for Schools and Coaching Institutes

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 2 November 2026

Parents and students often check a school or coaching institute on Instagram and Facebook before they call. These content ideas help you present your institute clearly and honestly.

1. Admission-season posts

  • Key dates and last dates to apply
  • Documents needed and how to apply, as a simple carousel
  • Clear batch, fee and timing details
  • Common admission questions answered by a staff member

2. Show your teaching

  • Teacher introductions, with qualifications you can verify
  • One-minute concept or doubt-solving clips
  • Study tips before exams
  • A short tour of classrooms, labs or the library

3. Show daily life

  • Events, sports day, assemblies and practical sessions
  • Behind-the-scenes moments from preparation
  • Festival wishes and important announcements

4. Build trust with parents

  • A facilities tour
  • Safety, transport and communication information
  • Highlights from parent meetings
  • Honest answers to questions parents often ask

5. Share achievements honestly

Celebrate results, but only with verified facts and the written consent of the student and parent. Say what the result was and in which year. Avoid selecting only the best cases in a way that creates a false impression.

6. Reels and short videos

  • A teacher tip of the week
  • A day at your institute
  • Exam-preparation checklists
  • Countdown reels before admissions or exams
  • Student voices, with consent

Get consent and avoid risky claims

Before you post photos or videos of children, get written permission from parents and follow your institute’s own child-safety policy. Do not show personal details such as phone numbers, addresses or individual marks without permission.

Be careful with claims in your advertising. In November 2024 India’s Central Consumer Protection Authority notified guidelines on misleading advertisements in the coaching sector, which prohibit claims such as guaranteed selection. Rules can change, so check the current guidelines and avoid exaggerated or unverifiable promises. This is general information and not legal advice.

A simple weekly plan (example)

  • Monday: a teacher tip or study idea
  • Wednesday: a student-life moment or event, with consent
  • Friday: a parent question answered, or an admission update
  • Once a week: one short reel

Adjust this to what your team can keep up every week. Consistency matters more than volume.

Measure what matters

Track admission enquiries that come from Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp, not only likes. Ask every enquiry how they heard about you, and note which type of post brings real conversations.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need consent before posting photos of students?

Yes. Get clear consent from parents or guardians before sharing photos or videos of children, follow your institution’s policy and any applicable rules, and avoid sharing sensitive details.

How often should a school or coaching institute post?

Choose a rhythm you can keep for at least 90 days. Consistency and usefulness matter more than daily posting.

Can we share student results?

Only with permission and with honest context. Avoid exaggerated claims and make clear what the result means.
