Social Media Marketing Guide for Small Businesses: From First Post to Steady Enquiries

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

Social media works best when it is planned, simple and tied to enquiries. This social media marketing guide walks you through the essentials.

Step 1: choose where to show up

You do not need every platform. Instagram suits visual businesses, Facebook works well for local communities and parents, and YouTube Shorts can reuse your reels. Choose one or two where your customers already are.

Step 2: choose content pillars

PillarExamples
EducationTips, myths, how-to clips
Behind the scenesTeam, process, workspace
ProofReal work with permission
OffersBatches, events, seasonal offers
CommunityQuestions, polls, answers

Step 3: set a rhythm you can keep

Choose a posting rhythm you can sustain for at least 90 days, and plan one month at a time. Batch your content: record and photograph once, then create several posts from it.

Step 4: engage, do not just broadcast

Reply to comments and messages, ask questions and answer them. Social media is a conversation, and people buy from businesses that respond.

Step 5: measure what matters

  • Saves, shares and profile visits.
  • Messages, calls and direction requests.
  • Enquiries that mention social media.

A worked example (concept)

A tutor chooses Instagram, picks education, behind-the-scenes and proof as pillars, and plans three posts and one reel a week for a month. After ninety days the tutor reviews which posts brought messages, keeps those formats and drops the rest.

A 30-day plan

  1. Week 1: choose platform and pillars.
  2. Week 2: create and schedule the first batch.
  3. Week 3: engage with every comment and message.
  4. Week 4: review and adjust.
