Less Dependence on Word of Mouth and a Single Channel

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

Referrals are valuable, but relying on them alone is risky. Here is how to add other sources without losing what works.

Key takeaways

  • Map your current sources
  • Make referrals easy
  • Build a Google profile and website
  • Choose one extra channel

What success looks like

  • Customers arrive from several sources.
  • A drop in referrals does not threaten the business.
  • New customers can find you without knowing someone.
  • Referrals are easier because people can check you online.

Why it matters to business owners

Word of mouth is trusted but unpredictable. Customers also search online before acting on a recommendation, so your online presence supports referrals.

What it realistically takes

Diversifying means building owned and shared assets: a website, a Google profile, a customer list and one or two content channels. It takes time and does not replace relationships.

How to move toward it

  1. Map your current sources.
  2. Make referrals easy: a short message or link people can share.
  3. Build a Google profile and website that confirm credibility.
  4. Choose one extra channel and do it consistently.
  5. Collect customer contacts with permission.
  6. Review the share of each source every quarter.

Numbers to watch

  • Share of customers from each source
  • Referral rate
  • New-customer enquiries from search
  • Repeat customers

A concept example

A salon relies on regulars. It adds a Google profile with photos and a simple shareable booking link, so new customers can check and book easily. This is an illustration, not a client result.

Mistakes that slow it down

  • Neglecting referrals while adding channels.
  • Spreading across too many channels.
  • Not collecting contact details.
  • Ignoring your online reputation.

Myths vs reality

MythReality
Digital marketing replaces word of mouthIt supports it
More channels means betterFocus and consistency matter
Customers do not check onlineMany do

A 30-day action plan

  1. Week 1: List where your last 20 customers came from.
  2. Week 2: Improve your Google profile.
  3. Week 3: Create a shareable booking or enquiry link.
  4. Week 4: Pick one additional channel.

How to measure improvement

Compare the share of customers from referrals and other sources over time. A healthier mix means less risk.

Frequently asked questions

Is word of mouth still important?

Yes. Online presence helps convert referrals and add new sources.

Which channel should I add?

Often Google Business Profile, then one content channel your customers use.

How do I keep referrals strong?

Deliver well, ask politely and make sharing simple.
