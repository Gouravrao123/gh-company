Google Ads Optimization Tips for Small Businesses: A Weekly Routine That Works

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

Optimization is not a secret setting. It is a habit of reviewing what people searched, what they saw and what they did next. Here are practical tips you can apply every week.

Start with the search terms report

The search terms report shows the real searches that triggered your ads. Review it weekly. Add irrelevant searches as negative keywords so you stop paying for them, and add good new searches as keywords. This one habit often improves results more than any other.

Google Ads optimization tips, in order of impact

  1. Use negative keywords. Block searches like jobs, free or unrelated services.
  2. Match keywords to intent. Specific, local and buyer-style searches usually beat broad ones.
  3. Write ads that mirror the search. Use the words people typed and a clear offer.
  4. Test two or three ad versions and keep the stronger one.
  5. Improve the landing page. Match the ad, load fast and show contact options.
  6. Check location and timing settings. Show ads where and when you can serve customers.
  7. Track real conversions, not only clicks.
  8. Adjust budgets slowly, not daily.

Common optimization mistakes

  • Changing many things at once so nothing can be learned.
  • Optimizing for clicks instead of enquiries.
  • Ignoring the landing page.
  • Pausing campaigns too early.

When to ask for help

A worked example (concept)

A coaching centre sees many clicks but few enquiries. In the search terms report it finds searches for free material and jobs. It adds those as negatives, rewrites the ad to mention batch timings and fees process, and moves clicks from the home page to a batch page with a WhatsApp button. Over the next weeks it compares cost per enquiry rather than clicks.

A weekly 30-minute routine

  1. Check spend against budget.
  2. Read new search terms and add negatives.
  3. Compare the two ad versions.
  4. Look at enquiries and cost per enquiry.
  5. Write one change for next week and note it.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I optimize my ads?

Review search terms and results weekly, and make small, planned changes rather than daily tweaks.

What is a negative keyword?

A word or phrase that tells Google not to show your ad for certain searches, such as free or jobs.

Why do I get clicks but no enquiries?

Common causes are mismatched keywords, a weak landing page or missing tracking.
