How to Set Up Your Google Business Profile in Rewari

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 2 November 2026

A Google Business Profile helps nearby customers find your business on Google Search and Maps. This guide explains how to set one up properly if you run a business in Rewari.

What a Google Business Profile does

It is a free profile that can show your business name, category, services, phone number, website, photos and customer reviews when people search for you or for a service near them. For many local searches it is one of the first things a customer sees, so it is worth getting right.

1. Check that your business is eligible

Google expects a business to be real and to deal with customers in person, either at a location or by visiting them. Use your real business name, not a name stuffed with keywords or locations. Do not use a PO box or a virtual office as your address.

2. Decide: shop or service area

If customers visit you at a shop, clinic, gym or office during set hours, you can show your address. If you work from home, or you mostly travel to customers or work online, Google says a service-area business should hide its address and show the areas it serves instead. Choose the areas you genuinely cover, such as Rewari and nearby towns. Google allows up to 20 service areas, but list only places you really serve.

3. Choose the right category and services

Pick the primary category that best describes your main business, because it tells Google what you do. Add other categories only if they are true. Then list your services in plain words your customers use, with a short and honest description.

4. Add accurate contact details and hours

Add your phone number and website. Add opening hours only if they are real, and update them for holidays. Write your name, address and phone number in exactly the same way on your website, Instagram and other listings. We cover this in our local SEO tips for businesses in Haryana.

5. Add real photos

Upload clear photos of your work, team, premises or products. Avoid stock images. Fresh photos show customers that the business is active.

6. Verify your profile

Google asks you to confirm that you control the business before the profile is fully available. Follow the steps Google shows you and use the verification method it offers.

7. Collect genuine reviews

Ask happy customers to review you after they have used your service, and reply politely to every review, including critical ones. Never buy reviews, write your own or offer rewards in exchange for reviews. This goes against Google’s policies and can get a profile removed.

8. Keep it updated and check the numbers

Add updates, offers and new photos regularly. Look at the calls, direction requests and website clicks shown for your profile to understand what customers do.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Creating several profiles for the same business
  • Choosing the wrong category, or too many categories
  • Adding keywords or locations to the business name
  • Using a fake address or a virtual office
  • Ignoring reviews and questions
  • Leaving old phone numbers or opening hours

What to expect

A complete and accurate profile gives you a better chance of appearing in local searches, but nobody can guarantee a position. Local results depend on relevance, distance and how well known your business is, and they build over time.

Frequently asked questions

Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Creating and managing a profile is free. You may choose to pay for ads separately, but the profile itself costs nothing.

Can I have a profile if I work from home or visit customers?

Eligibility depends on Google’s rules for service-area businesses. Follow the guidance in this guide and Google’s own instructions, and do not use an address where you have no real presence.

How long does verification take?

It varies. Follow the steps Google gives you and keep your details accurate. If verification fails, check that your information matches your real business.
