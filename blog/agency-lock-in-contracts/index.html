Agency Lock-In Contracts: What to Check Before You Sign

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

Six or twelve months before you have seen how an agency works is a big commitment. Here is what to check in any contract.

Key takeaways

  • Ask the minimum term
  • Check the notice period
  • Ask what you will receive if you leave
  • Check renewal terms

What this frustration looks like

  • You are asked to commit for a long period immediately.
  • Exit terms are vague or missing.
  • Notice periods are long.
  • You do not know what happens to your files if you leave.

Why it happens

Agencies like predictable income, and some work genuinely needs time, such as SEO. But long terms without a clear scope or exit shift risk to the client.

What it costs you

If the fit is poor, you pay for months of work you do not want. Disputes over leaving can be stressful and expensive.

How to avoid it

  1. Ask the minimum term and why it is needed.
  2. Check the notice period and how to end the agreement.
  3. Ask what you will receive if you leave: files, access, reports.
  4. Check renewal terms. Avoid automatic renewals you may forget.
  5. Consider a shorter start, such as a one-month or three-month trial of scope.
  6. Read refund and cancellation clauses carefully.

Questions to ask any agency

  • What is the minimum term and why?
  • How do I end the agreement, and with how much notice?
  • What do I keep if I leave?
  • Is there an automatic renewal?

A concept example

A shop agrees to twelve months for SEO but wants to stop at four months because of poor communication. A three-month start would have limited the risk. This is an illustration, not a client result.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Signing without reading.
  • Not checking auto-renewal.
  • Assuming you can leave anytime.
  • Skipping questions about ownership of work.

Myths vs reality

MythReality
Long contracts mean better resultsResults come from work quality, not contract length
Short terms are unprofessionalMany good agencies start with shorter terms
Cancellation is always simpleCheck the clause

A 30-day action plan

  1. Week 1: Request the agreement in writing.
  2. Week 2: Highlight the term, notice and renewal.
  3. Week 3: Ask questions about anything unclear.
  4. Week 4: Negotiate a trial period if you are unsure.

How to measure improvement

You will know it works when you can state the term, the notice period and what happens at the end without looking at the document.

Frequently asked questions

Is a long contract normal for SEO?

SEO often takes time, so longer terms are common, but you should still understand exit terms.

Can I negotiate the term?

Often yes. Ask for a shorter start or a review point.

What should I keep if I leave?

Content, files and access to accounts that belong to your business.
