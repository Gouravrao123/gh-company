Agency Reports Full of Likes and Reach but No Enquiries: What to Ask For

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

A colourful monthly report can still leave you asking one question: did my business get more customers? Here is how to get reports that answer it.

Key takeaways

  • Agree your goal numbers at the start
  • Ask for the source of each enquiry
  • Set up simple tracking
  • Ask for a short explanation

What this frustration looks like

  • Reports show reach, impressions and follower growth only.
  • You cannot tell how many calls or messages came from marketing.
  • Charts look impressive but nobody explains them.
  • The next month’s plan is not clear.

Why it happens

Platform numbers like reach and likes are easy to export, so they fill reports. Business outcomes need tracking to be set up, which takes planning. Without agreed goals at the start, the report shows whatever is available.

What it costs you

If reports do not show business results, you cannot decide where to invest, and the agency cannot prove or improve its work. You may keep paying for activity without knowing whether it helps.

How to avoid it

  1. Agree your goal numbers at the start: calls, messages, forms, visits, admissions.
  2. Ask for the source of each enquiry, such as ad, search, social post or referral.
  3. Set up simple tracking, including a question to every new customer: how did you find us?
  4. Ask for a short explanation with every report: what happened and why.
  5. Include costs. If ads run, report spend and cost per enquiry.
  6. End every report with next steps and any decisions you need to make.

Questions to ask any agency

  • Which three to five numbers will you report?
  • How will you track enquiries from each channel?
  • Which numbers are signals and which are results?
  • What will you change next month based on this report?

A concept example

A coaching centre sees 40,000 reach and 200 new followers but cannot say how many parents enquired. A better report would list enquiries by source and the cost of the ads that produced them. This is an illustration, not a client result.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Treating followers as customers.
  • Accepting reports without a review call.
  • Not tracking offline enquiries.
  • Comparing months without noting seasonal changes.

Myths vs reality

MythReality
More reach means more businessReach shows visibility, not intent
Long reports are betterClear reports answer your questions briefly
Only the agency can understand reportsGood reports are explained in plain language

A 30-day action plan

  1. Week 1: Write down the results that matter to you.
  2. Week 2: Share them with your agency and agree how they will be tracked.
  3. Week 3: Hold a review call after the next report.
  4. Week 4: Compare the following month with the first one.

How to measure improvement

You will know reporting works when you can say, after reading it, how many enquiries came, from where, at what cost and what will change next month.

Frequently asked questions

Which numbers should an agency report?

Match them to your goals: calls, messages, forms, direction requests, website visits and, for ads, cost per result.

Are impressions useless?

No, they show visibility. But on their own they do not show business results.

How often should I receive reports?

Monthly is common, with short updates for active campaigns.
