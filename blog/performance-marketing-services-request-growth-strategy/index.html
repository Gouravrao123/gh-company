Performance Marketing Services Explained, and How to Request a Growth Strategy

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 6 November 2026

Performance marketing focuses on measurable actions: calls, form fills, WhatsApp messages and sales. This guide explains performance marketing services and how to request a growth strategy for your business.

In this guide

What performance marketing is

It is paid and tracked marketing where every campaign is tied to a result you can count. It usually includes Google Ads, Meta ads, landing pages and tracking. It does not mean guaranteed results; it means clear numbers. Start with Google Ads basics if you are new.

What performance marketing services include

  1. Goal and tracking setup. Calls, forms and WhatsApp clicks measured properly. See why ads without tracking waste money.
  2. Campaigns. Search, social and remarketing, matched to your goal and budget.
  3. Landing pages. Pages built to turn clicks into enquiries. See how to improve ad conversion rate.
  4. Optimisation. Regular reviews of search terms, audiences and creative.
  5. Reporting. Plain-language reports on enquiries and cost per enquiry.

Budget and expectations

Start with a budget you can afford to test for a few months. Our guides on ad budget for small businesses and Meta ads budget in Haryana give practical starting points.

Request a growth strategy

To request growth strategy support, tell us your business, location, goal and current marketing. We will review where you are, suggest priorities across SEO, social and ads, and explain the next steps and costs in writing. There is no pressure to commit. Use the button below or see how to request a marketing proposal.

What helps us help you

Your goal (for example, 30 enquiries a month), your service area, your average sale value and access to your website and ad accounts. See also Meta Ads Management.

Frequently asked questions

What are performance marketing services?

Tracked advertising and conversion work, such as Google and Meta ads and landing pages, measured by calls, forms and sales.

Can you guarantee leads?

No honest agency can. We commit to clear setup, tracking and reporting, and to improving results over time.

How do I request a growth strategy?

Use the contact form or WhatsApp with your goal and business details. We will reply with a written plan.
