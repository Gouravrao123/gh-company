Website Design Best Practices for Small Businesses: Clear, Fast and Easy to Contact

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

A good website is not about decoration. It is about helping a visitor decide quickly. Here are the website design best practices we use, and how to request a website design quote.

Website design best practices

  1. Say what you do in the first screen. A clear headline, a supporting line and one button.
  2. Design for mobile first. Most visitors arrive on a phone.
  3. Keep navigation simple. A few clear links beat a crowded menu.
  4. Make contact easy: visible call and WhatsApp buttons and a short form.
  5. Use real photos of your place, team and work.
  6. Keep text readable: good size, contrast and short paragraphs.
  7. Make it fast. Compress images and avoid heavy sliders.
  8. Show trust: honest reviews, clear policies and contact details.
  9. Build SEO in: titles, headings, descriptions and internal links.
  10. Test and improve based on real enquiries.

A worked example (concept)

A repair shop’s old site has a slideshow, a long menu and a tiny phone number in the footer. A redesign keeps one headline, three services, a visible call button and photos of real work. Pages load faster, text is larger on phones, and each service has its own page. The owner tracks calls from the site before and after.

A pre-launch checklist

  1. Test on two different phones.
  2. Check every button and form.
  3. Check page titles and descriptions.
  4. Check speed and image sizes.
  5. Confirm contact details and policies.
  6. Set up tracking and a sitemap.

Frequently asked questions

How many pages should a small business website have?

Usually a home page, one page per main service, an about page and a contact page. Add more only when you have useful things to say.
