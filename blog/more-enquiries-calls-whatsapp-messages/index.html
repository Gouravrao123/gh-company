How to Get More Enquiries: Calls, WhatsApp Messages and Forms

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

More enquiries is the first thing almost every owner wants. Here is what it takes, in what order, and what is realistic to expect.

Key takeaways

  • Make the offer clear
  • Make contact effortless
  • Be visible where customers search
  • Give people a reason to act now

What success looks like

  • The phone and WhatsApp ring more often with people who actually need your service.
  • Enquiry forms are filled by relevant visitors.
  • You can say where each enquiry came from.
  • Busy periods are no longer a surprise.

Why it matters to business owners

Enquiries are the bridge between marketing and sales. Visibility and likes mean little if nobody contacts you, and owners rightly measure marketing by whether the phone rings. At the same time, more enquiries only help if you can handle them well.

What it realistically takes

Three things must work together: people need to see you, they need to understand and trust your offer, and contacting you must be easy. If any link is weak, enquiries stay low. Results usually build over weeks and months, not days, and they depend on your offer, price, location and competition.

How to move toward it

  1. Make the offer clear. One sentence on who you help and with what.
  2. Make contact effortless. Call and WhatsApp buttons on every page and profile.
  3. Be visible where customers search: a complete Google profile and service pages.
  4. Give people a reason to act now: a clear next step, limited batches or a free consultation, honestly stated.
  5. Reply quickly and keep answers helpful.
  6. Track every enquiry and note its source.

Numbers to watch

  • Total enquiries per week and per month
  • Enquiries by source (search, social, ads, referral)
  • Share of enquiries that become customers
  • Time to first reply

A concept example

A tuition centre improves its website headline, adds a WhatsApp button and answers messages within an hour. It cannot promise numbers, but each change removes friction between interest and contact. This is an illustration, not a client result.

Mistakes that slow it down

  • Counting likes as enquiries.
  • Making people fill long forms.
  • Hiding phone numbers.
  • Spending on ads before fixing the contact path.

Myths vs reality

MythReality
More traffic means more enquiriesOnly relevant, well-converted traffic does
Ads are the only waySearch, profile and referrals matter too
Enquiries are out of my controlClarity, speed and visibility are in your control

A 30-day action plan

  1. Week 1: Check that every page has a call and WhatsApp option.
  2. Week 2: Rewrite your main offer and update your Google profile.
  3. Week 3: Set up a simple enquiry sheet with source and status.
  4. Week 4: Review numbers and fix the biggest drop-off.

How to measure improvement

Compare weekly enquiries before and after each change. Even a small rise in relevant enquiries, with faster replies, shows the system is improving.

Frequently asked questions

How many enquiries should I expect?

It depends on your offer, location, price and competition. Track your own baseline and aim for steady improvement.

Are WhatsApp enquiries better than calls?

Not better, just different. Offer both and reply quickly on each.

How soon can I see more enquiries?

Simple fixes can help within weeks. Search visibility usually takes longer.
