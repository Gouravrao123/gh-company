Agency Promising Guaranteed Results? Why Big Promises Are a Red Flag

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

“First page of Google in 30 days” and “guaranteed leads” sound exciting. Here is why such promises should make you cautious and what an honest agency says instead.

Key takeaways

  • Ask what the agency controls
  • Ask how a result would be achieved
  • Ask about your competition and market
  • Prefer commitments on work and reporting

What this frustration looks like

  • The agency guarantees a Google ranking or a fixed number of leads.
  • Results are promised before anyone has looked at your website or market.
  • The promise comes with pressure to sign quickly.
  • No explanation of how the result will be achieved.

Why it happens

Promises sell. A confident guarantee is easier to market than an honest explanation of effort, uncertainty and time. Some agencies use guarantees to win the sale and deal with the consequences later, because the customer has already paid.

What it costs you

A guarantee that cannot be kept leaves you with lost months, a damaged budget and distrust of digital marketing as a whole. In some cases the shortcuts used to chase a promised ranking can even harm your website.

How to avoid it

  1. Ask what the agency controls. They control their work, not Google’s algorithm or your customers’ decisions.
  2. Ask how a result would be achieved. Look for a clear plan, not a slogan.
  3. Ask about your competition and market. Honest answers depend on them.
  4. Prefer commitments on work and reporting: deliverables, timelines, review dates.
  5. Ask what happens if results are slower than hoped.
  6. Get any promise in writing, including what is excluded.

Questions to ask any agency

  • What exactly do you commit to delivering each month?
  • What parts of the outcome are outside your control?
  • How will we know in 30, 60 and 90 days whether the plan is on track?
  • What happens if progress is slower than expected?

A concept example

A restaurant owner is told “top 3 on Google in a month”. A better answer would be: “We will fix your profile, create service pages and review progress monthly, and we cannot promise a position.” This is an illustration, not a client result.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Choosing the agency with the boldest promise.
  • Believing a guarantee because of a refund clause you have not read.
  • Ignoring how the result will be produced.
  • Skipping the written scope.

Myths vs reality

MythReality
A guarantee shows confidenceNobody outside the search engine can guarantee rankings; confidence should show in process and transparency
Higher price means safer promisePrice and honesty are unrelated
Refund clauses protect meThey often have conditions that are hard to meet

A 30-day action plan

  1. Week 1: List the promises you have heard from agencies so far.
  2. Week 2: Rewrite each as a question about process and evidence.
  3. Week 3: Ask two agencies the same questions and compare the answers.
  4. Week 4: Choose the partner whose answers are specific and honest.

How to measure improvement

Success here means you understand exactly what you are buying, what is not guaranteed and how progress will be shown. If you can explain the plan to a friend in two minutes, you are in a good position.

Frequently asked questions

Can anyone guarantee a Google ranking?

No. Search engines decide rankings, and they change often. Be careful with anyone who guarantees a position.

Is a performance-based fee a guarantee?

Not necessarily. Read exactly what is counted, what is excluded and who controls the numbers.

What can an agency honestly promise?

The work it will do, the reporting you will receive and clear communication. We do not guarantee rankings, leads or sales.
