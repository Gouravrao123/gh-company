SEO vs PPC Services: Which Should a Small Business Start With?

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

SEO builds lasting visibility, while paid ads can bring quicker enquiries. Here is how to choose, and when to combine them.

The short answer

If you need enquiries soon, begin with a small, tracked paid test and fix your website basics at the same time. If you want lasting visibility, add SEO. Many businesses eventually use both, because they do different jobs.

Why business owners ask this

SEO and PPC are often presented as rivals. Owners with limited money want to know where to start so they do not waste it.

What affects the answer

The choice depends on urgency, budget, competition, how searchable your service is and how ready your website is. Some services are searched often, which suits SEO. Others rely on discovery, which suits social ads.

Step by step: find your own answer

  1. Check whether people search for your service.
  2. Decide how soon you need enquiries.
  3. Check your website readiness before paying for traffic.
  4. Set a budget for each if using both.
  5. Test paid ads in small steps.
  6. Build SEO in parallel with local profile and service pages.

Useful things to check

  • Cost per enquiry from ads
  • Search impressions and clicks
  • Enquiries from each channel
  • Combined cost per customer

A concept example

A new gym runs a small Meta test for trial sign-ups and builds a Google profile and service pages in parallel for steady local visibility. This is an illustration, not a client result.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Doing SEO and ads with a broken website.
  • Expecting SEO speed from SEO.
  • Stopping ads and expecting the traffic to stay.
  • Not tracking separately.

Myths vs reality

MythReality
SEO is freeIt costs time and skill
Ads always bring buyersThey bring clicks that need a good offer

A 30-day action plan

  1. Week 1: Check search interest and website readiness.
  2. Week 2: Pick your main goal.
  3. Week 3: Launch a small test or SEO basics.
  4. Week 4: Review results at 30 and 90 days.

How to measure improvement

Compare enquiries and cost from each channel monthly. Use the data to shift your mix.

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper?

It depends on competition and scope. Ads have ongoing spend, SEO has ongoing work.

Can I do both?

Yes, many businesses do.

Which is better for local businesses?

Local SEO and a Google profile often work well, with ads for faster tests.
