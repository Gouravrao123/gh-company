Mistake: Not Tracking Anything

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

If you cannot count calls, messages and forms, you cannot tell what works. Here is how to start tracking simply.

Key takeaways

  • Define what counts as a result
  • Create a simple sheet
  • Ask every new customer how they found you
  • Set up Search Console and profile insights

What this mistake looks like

  • No record of where enquiries come from.
  • Decisions are based on feelings.
  • Reports are not checked or do not exist.
  • You cannot say what an enquiry costs.

Why businesses make it

Tracking feels technical and time-consuming. When business is busy, it is dropped.

What it costs you

Without tracking you may keep spending on what fails and cut what works. You also cannot show value or improve.

What to do instead

  1. Define what counts as a result.
  2. Create a simple sheet with date, source and status.
  3. Ask every new customer how they found you.
  4. Set up Search Console and profile insights.
  5. Use separate links or numbers where possible.
  6. Review monthly.

A quick self-check

  • Do I know my weekly enquiries?
  • Can I name my top source?
  • Do I know my cost per enquiry?
  • Am I checking my numbers monthly?

A concept example

A clinic starts asking patients how they found it and sees that local search brings more bookings than social posts. This is an illustration, not a client result.

Related mistakes

  • Tracking only likes.
  • Forgetting offline sources.
  • Not recording outcomes.
  • Never reviewing.

Worked example: a one-page tracking sheet

Create a table with columns for date, name or number, how they found you, what they wanted, status and outcome. Fill it in for every enquiry for a month. At the end, count by source and by outcome. You will often find that one or two sources bring most customers, and that some enquiries never received a reply.

That single page tells you where to put effort and where the follow-up is failing. It takes minutes a day and costs nothing, which is why it is the best first investment in measurement.

A 30-day action plan

  1. Week 1: Create your sheet.
  2. Week 2: Start asking the source question.
  3. Week 3: Set up free tools.
  4. Week 4: Review at month end.

How to measure improvement

Success is being able to state last month’s enquiries by source.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest tracking method?

A sheet recording each enquiry and its source.

Which free tools help?

Search Console, profile insights and platform reports.

How often should I review?

Weekly for campaigns, monthly for planning.
