How Do You Know Your Digital Marketing Is Working?

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

Likes are easy to see, but enquiries and customers matter more. Here is a simple way to check results.

Key takeaways

  • Record your baseline
  • Define results
  • Track sources
  • Ask customers how they found you

The short answer

Look at enquiries first: calls, WhatsApp messages, forms and visits. Then check supporting numbers such as profile actions, search clicks and website visits.

Why business owners ask this

Owners spend money and want evidence.

What affects the answer

Evidence needs tracking, a baseline and patience. Different channels show results at different speeds.

Step by step: find your own answer

  1. Record your baseline.
  2. Define results.
  3. Track sources.
  4. Ask customers how they found you.
  5. Review monthly.
  6. Act on the findings.

Useful things to check

  • Enquiries by source
  • Cost per enquiry
  • Profile actions
  • Search impressions and clicks

A concept example

A clinic tracks calls from its profile, forms from its site and mentions of Instagram, and finds that local search brings most appointments. This is an illustration, not a client result.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Relying on likes.
  • No baseline.
  • Ignoring offline results.
  • Changing too much.

Worked example: a simple monthly check

A clinic records three numbers on the first of each month: calls from its Google profile, appointment forms from its website and bookings that mention social media. In month one the numbers are 18, 6 and 3. By month three they are 25, 9 and 4. These figures are illustrative, but the habit is real: one baseline, three numbers and a short note about what changed.

Alongside the numbers, the clinic writes one line: “Added service pages in month two.” Over time the notes link actions to results, which is exactly what you need to decide where to put the next rupee.

Myths vs reality

MythReality
Followers show successEnquiries do
Tracking is technicalA sheet works
One month tells allTrends matter

A 30-day action plan

  1. Week 1: Record baselines.
  2. Week 2: Set up a sheet.
  3. Week 3: Review weekly.
  4. Week 4: Decide monthly.

How to measure improvement

If you can say what came in, from where and at what cost, you can tell whether it is working.

Frequently asked questions

What is the simplest tracking method?

A sheet with date, source and status of each enquiry.

Which tools help?

Search Console, profile insights and platform reports.

How often should I review?

Weekly for campaigns, monthly for strategy.
