Paid Social Strategy for Local Businesses: Facebook and Instagram Ads That Make Sense

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

Paid social lets you reach people near you before they start searching. A simple strategy keeps spending focused. Here is how to build one.

What paid social is

Paid social means advertising on social platforms, such as Facebook and Instagram. You choose an audience by location, interests and behaviour, and show them ads in feeds, stories and reels. It works well for discovery, offers and reminders.

A simple paid social strategy

StageGoalWhat to show
AwarenessReach new people nearbyShort video, strong visual, simple local message
ConsiderationHelp interested people learn moreService explainers, FAQs, proof
ConversionGet an enquiryClear offer, WhatsApp or form button, retargeting

Steps to plan your first campaign

  1. Choose one goal such as WhatsApp messages or bookings.
  2. Define a local audience and who it is for.
  3. Prepare two or three creatives with a clear offer.
  4. Send people to a ready page or chat.
  5. Set a small test budget over a few weeks.
  6. Track enquiries and cost.
  7. Review weekly, pause the weakest and scale the strongest slowly.

Creative that works locally

  • Real people, places and work from your business.
  • Short videos with captions and Hinglish subtitles where suitable.
  • One idea per ad.
  • A clear next step.

Paid social and organic content together

Organic posts build familiarity and trust, and ads put your best content in front of more people. Run ads on content that already gets good reactions. Read our Social Media Management page for how we plan both.

A worked example (concept)

A salon plans three steps. For awareness it runs a short reel to people within a few kilometres. For consideration it shows a carousel about services and a customer question answered. For conversion it shows an offer to people who watched the reel, with a WhatsApp button. It tracks messages and bookings and keeps only the creatives that bring conversations.

Mistakes to avoid

Targeting too wide an audience, using one creative for every stage, sending clicks to a profile with no action button and judging results after two days. Fixing these usually improves results more than raising the budget.

Keep a simple log of audience, creative, budget and result for each test. After a month you will know what your local audience responds to.

Frequently asked questions

Is paid social better than search ads?

They do different jobs. Search ads reach people who are already searching, and paid social reaches people who may not know they need you yet.

What budget should I start with?

A small amount you can afford to lose while learning, spread over a few weeks.

How do I know paid social is working?

Count enquiries and cost per enquiry, not only likes and reach.
