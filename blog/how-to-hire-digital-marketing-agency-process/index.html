How to Hire a Digital Marketing Agency: The Process and How to Request an SEO Proposal

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

A healthy hiring process has clear steps. Here is how it should look and how to ask for a written SEO proposal.

Key takeaways

The short answer

A healthy process starts with a first conversation, then a review of your current presence, a written proposal, agreement on scope and price, and regular delivery and reporting.

Why business owners ask this

Owners often do not know what to expect or what to ask.

What affects the answer

Quality depends on discovery, clarity of scope, communication and fit. Proposals vary by agency, but a good one reflects your goals.

Step by step: find your own answer

  1. Define your goal.
  2. Shortlist two or three agencies.
  3. Hold discovery calls.
  4. Request a written proposal.
  5. Compare scope, price and terms.
  6. Start with a clear first-month plan.

Useful things to check

  • Understanding of your business
  • Clarity of scope
  • Quality of questions asked
  • Responsiveness

A concept example

A school asks three agencies for proposals. One asks detailed questions and provides a plan that reflects their situation. This is an illustration, not a client result.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Skipping the proposal.
  • Choosing on price alone.
  • Not asking about terms.
  • Ignoring communication.

What a good request for an SEO proposal looks like

A useful request is short and specific: “We run a coaching institute in Rewari. Our website is [link]. We teach Class 9 to 12 Maths and Science. Our main competitors are [names]. We want more admission enquiries before the next session. Please send a written SEO proposal with priorities, deliverables, timeline and price.”

With that information a good agency can review your site, find quick fixes and bigger gaps, and respond with something tailored. If a reply arrives instantly with a generic package, it is a sign the proposal was not written for you.

A 30-day action plan

  1. Week 1: Define your goals.
  2. Week 2: Request proposals.
  3. Week 3: Compare.
  4. Week 4: Decide and start.

How to measure improvement

Check that the first month’s deliverables match the proposal.

Frequently asked questions

How do I request an SEO proposal from you?

Send your website link, main services, city or area and competitors, and choose SEO Proposal Request on the contact form.

What does a proposal include?

Goals, deliverables, timeline, price, exclusions and measurement.

How long does it take?

Usually a few days after discovery.
