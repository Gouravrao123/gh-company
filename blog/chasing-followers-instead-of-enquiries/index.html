Mistake: Chasing Followers Instead of Enquiries

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

A large following that never contacts you is a vanity metric. Here is how to focus on what builds the business.

Key takeaways

  • Set goals around enquiries
  • Create content that answers buying questions
  • Add clear calls to action
  • Track profile visits and messages

What this mistake looks like

  • Celebrating follower counts.
  • Content designed for likes only.
  • Few enquiries despite many followers.
  • No tracking of messages or calls.

Why businesses make it

Follower counts are visible and easy to compare. They feel like a score.

What it costs you

Time and money go to attention that does not convert.

What to do instead

  1. Set goals around enquiries.
  2. Create content that answers buying questions.
  3. Add clear calls to action.
  4. Track profile visits and messages.
  5. Engage with local people.
  6. Review conversions monthly.

A quick self-check

  • How many enquiries came from social?
  • Which posts led to messages?
  • Are followers local?
  • Do posts have clear actions?

A concept example

A tutor shifts from viral topics to parent questions and sees more messages. This is an illustration, not a client result.

Related mistakes

  • Buying followers.
  • Copying trends.
  • Ignoring comments.
  • Not tracking.

Worked example: from followers to actions

A tutor with a few thousand followers notices that most reels about trending topics get likes but no messages. After switching to posts answering parent questions, with a clear “message us” prompt, likes fall slightly but messages rise. The tutor tracks messages per post and keeps the formats that bring conversations.

The change is not about posting more. It is about choosing content that moves a viewer to act, and measuring that action instead of the audience size.

Myths vs reality

MythReality
Followers equal customersOnly some do
Viral is the goalRelevant is better
Engagement is enoughActions matter

A 30-day action plan

  1. Week 1: Set an enquiry goal.
  2. Week 2: Plan useful posts.
  3. Week 3: Add calls to action.
  4. Week 4: Review monthly.

How to measure improvement

Track enquiries and actions from social.
