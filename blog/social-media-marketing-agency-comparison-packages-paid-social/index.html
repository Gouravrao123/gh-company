Social Media Marketing Agency Comparison: Packages, Paid Social Services and What to Ask

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 6 November 2026

Choosing a social media agency is easier when you know what to compare. This guide covers a social media marketing agency comparison, social media management packages and paid social services.

In this guide

Social media marketing agency comparison Social media management packages Paid social services Questions to ask any agency How we work Frequently asked questions

Social media marketing agency comparison

  1. Scope. What exactly is included each month: posts, reels, stories, replies, reports?
  2. Process. Who plans, who approves and how fast are changes made?
  3. Reporting. Are results shown as enquiries or only likes? See why likes-only reports mislead.
  4. Ownership. Your pages and ad accounts should stay in your name.
  5. Contracts. Check lock-in terms. See agency lock-in contracts.

Social media management packages

Packages usually bundle a set number of posts per month, design, captions, scheduling and reporting. Compare the numbers and the deliverables, not only the price. Ask what is extra (shoots, ad spend, video editing). Our pricing page explains how we price, and unclear deliverables shows what to watch for.

Paid social services

Paid social services run Facebook and Instagram ads on top of organic posting. A good setup defines the goal, audience, creative, budget and tracking before launch. Read our paid social strategy for local businesses and see Meta Ads Management. Ad spend is paid to the platform and is separate from agency fees.

Questions to ask any agency

  • Can I see a sample plan and report?
  • Who will work on my account?
  • What is the notice period?
  • How do you measure success?
  • What happens to my accounts if I leave?

How we work

We start with your goal, agree a clear scope in writing and report on enquiries. See Social Media Management or our social media marketing guide.

Frequently asked questions

What do social media management packages include?

Usually a set number of posts, design, captions, scheduling and reporting. Ask what is extra.

Is ad spend included in agency fees?

Normally not. Ad spend goes to the platform; the agency fee covers setup, management and reporting.

How do I compare social media agencies?

Compare scope, process, reporting, account ownership and contract terms side by side.
