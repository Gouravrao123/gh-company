How to Plan a Meta Ads Budget for a Local Business in Haryana

By GH Company, Rewari · Published 2 November 2026

Facebook and Instagram ads can bring enquiries to a local business, but only when the budget is planned around a clear goal. Here is a simple way to decide how much to spend and how to judge the results.

1. Start with one clear goal

Decide what you want: WhatsApp messages, phone calls, form enquiries or visits to your shop. Choose one main goal for each campaign. A budget split across several goals is hard to judge.

2. Work out what an enquiry is worth

Start with what a new customer is worth to you in profit, not revenue. Then estimate how many enquiries turn into customers. Multiplying the two gives you the most you can sensibly pay for one enquiry.

For illustration only, and not a benchmark: if a customer brings you ₹10,000 in profit and one in ten enquiries becomes a customer, an enquiry is worth about ₹1,000 to you. Your own numbers will be different, so use your own records.

3. Set a test budget you can afford

Choose an amount you can spend over a few weeks without stress, even if the first results are weak. Treat it as a test. Starting small and learning is better than spending a large amount on an untested ad. Decide the start date and the review date before you begin.

4. Keep the audience local and simple

Target the area your customers really come from, such as Rewari and a sensible distance around it, or the Haryana towns you serve. Use the language your customers use. Very narrow targeting can limit how many people see your ad.

5. Give people a clear offer and an easy next step

Use a clear offer, real photos or a short video, and one simple action such as messaging you on WhatsApp. Ads that open a WhatsApp chat can suit local businesses because customers already use the app every day.

6. Decide how you will count results

Before the ads start, agree how you will count enquiries: WhatsApp messages, calls or form submissions. Ask every new enquiry how they found you. Reply quickly, because slow replies waste ad spend.

7. Review weekly and change slowly

Check the cost per enquiry and, more importantly, the quality of the enquiries. Clicks and likes alone do not pay your bills. Give the ads time before making big changes, pause what clearly does not work and increase spend gradually on what does.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Boosting posts without a goal
  • Running many tiny campaigns at once
  • Judging results after two days
  • An unclear offer or a slow follow-up
  • Ignoring Meta’s advertising policies, especially for health or financial claims

Agency fees and ad spend

Ad spend is paid to Meta and is separate from any agency fee. Ask what the fee includes before you agree, and never accept a promise of guaranteed sales.
