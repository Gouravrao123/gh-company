“My Customers Are Not Online”: Why They Often Are

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

Even customers who do not follow brands often search, check maps and read messages. Here is how to test the belief.

Key takeaways

  • Ask your customers
  • Check profile insights
  • Offer phone and WhatsApp
  • Keep details accurate

How this belief sounds

  • “My customers are older or local.”
  • “They do not use social media.”
  • “They prefer calling.”

Where it comes from

Owners see customers face to face and assume they are offline.

What it costs you

You may miss searches, maps views and messages that happen before the visit.

A truer way to look at it

  1. Ask your customers how they found you.
  2. Check profile insights.
  3. Offer phone and WhatsApp.
  4. Keep details accurate.
  5. Test one online channel.
  6. Review the data.

Small first steps

  • How did your last ten customers find you?
  • Do they use WhatsApp?
  • Do they use Maps?

A concept example

A hardware shop learns many customers check Maps for directions and opening hours before visiting. This is an illustration, not a client result.

What to avoid

  • Assuming without asking.
  • Ignoring Maps.
  • Not offering WhatsApp.
  • Skipping tracking.

Worked example: asking customers directly

A hardware shop asks its next ten customers three questions: how did you find us, did you check anything online before visiting and would you message us on WhatsApp? Several mention Google Maps for timings and directions, and a few ask for WhatsApp price checks.

The owner had assumed customers were offline. The answers lead to a complete Maps listing and a WhatsApp number on the signboard, two small steps that match how people actually behave.

A 30-day action plan

  1. Week 1: Ask ten customers.
  2. Week 2: Check your profile.
  3. Week 3: Add WhatsApp.
  4. Week 4: Review after a month.

How to measure improvement

Compare profile actions and mentions of online sources.
