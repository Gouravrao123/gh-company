Local SEO Company Comparison, Pricing and How to Hire an SEO Agency

By Gourav Yadav, GH Company, Rewari · Published 4 November 2026

Choosing an SEO partner is hard because the work is hard to see. This guide shows how to compare local SEO companies, what pricing looks like and how to hire with confidence.

Hire SEO agency or SEO company: what to look for

  • A process that starts with an audit and your goals.
  • Plain-language explanations, not jargon.
  • Reporting that includes enquiries, not only rankings.
  • Clear scope, terms and ownership of your accounts.
  • Honesty: no guaranteed rankings.

Hire SEO company support: steps

  1. Define your goals and service area.
  2. Shortlist two or three providers.
  3. Ask the same questions to each.
  4. Request a written proposal.
  5. Compare scope, terms and communication.
  6. Start with a clear first-month plan.

A worked example (concept)

A clinic compares three providers. The first promises a ranking and cannot explain how. The second explains an audit, reports calls and direction requests and leaves the profile in the clinic’s name. The third quotes a very low price with no scope. The clinic builds a table and chooses the second.

What a first-month SEO plan looks like

  1. Audit of site, profile and competitors.
  2. Fixes to titles, pages and details.
  3. A list of priority pages and content.
  4. A tracking setup and a baseline report.
